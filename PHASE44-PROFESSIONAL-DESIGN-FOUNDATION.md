# Phase 44 — Professional Design Foundation

Phase 44 replaces quantity-first template generation with a reusable professional design foundation.

## Included
- Design tokens for spacing, typography, radii, elevation, and curated palettes.
- Twelve-column grid helpers.
- Template normalization and evidence-based quality auditing.
- Real SVG rendering in Template Center thumbnails.
- Restored and integrated premium templates from the user's Phase 24.3D project.
- The Phase 43.4.3 flagship party design preserved as the lead design.
- Searchable, editable template collection connected to the real Publisher editor.

## Templates
The library contains the flagship party template plus the eight original Phase 24.3D premium templates: real estate flyer, church program, healthcare brochure, school newsletter, event poster, restaurant menu, fashion lookbook, and technology pitch deck.
