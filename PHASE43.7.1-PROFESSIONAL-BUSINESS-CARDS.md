# Phase 43.7.1 — Professional Business Cards

This phase adds 20 original, fully editable front-and-back business card templates using the Phase 24.3D template architecture.

## Included
- 20 unique template IDs and names
- 40 editable PublisherPage objects (front and back)
- Editable PublisherElement text, shapes, image placeholders, QR placeholders, and decorative geometry
- Standard 3.5 × 2 inch business-card page size
- Professional font pairings and curated four-color palettes
- High-resolution no-people Unsplash photo placeholders
- New top-level Business Cards category in the Phase 43 template browser
- Updated category counts and template-search integration

## Quality rules
- No duplicate template IDs
- No duplicate template names
- Distinct geometric composition for every design
- Consistent margins, spacing, and typography hierarchy
- Preserves all previous Phase 43 templates
