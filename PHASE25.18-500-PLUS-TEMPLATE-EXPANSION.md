# Phase 25.18 - 500+ Template Expansion

Phase 25.18 expands the registered professional template library with 507 additional editable templates across 39 subcategories and 13 visual systems. Combined with the existing Phase 25 template collections, the active registry now contains more than 600 templates.

## Included
- 507 new templates
- 39 subcategories
- 13 palettes and six layout treatments
- Editable text, shapes, colors, tables, fields, and multipage documents
- No person photography
- Searchable metadata, category, industry, access, style, palette, and tags
