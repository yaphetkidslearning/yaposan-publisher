# Phase 28 — AI Video Studio

Integrated a provider-ready AI video workspace into the real Yaposan application.

## Included
- Image-to-video
- Text-to-video
- Talking-photo jobs
- AI presenter/avatar preparation
- Cinematic parallax and camera motion
- Product-video workflows
- Video upscale jobs
- Automatic highlight extraction
- Duration and aspect-ratio controls
- Generation queue and Motion Studio handoff model

Cloud generation requires a configured AI video provider. The workspace, state model, job queue, navigation and integration are included now.
