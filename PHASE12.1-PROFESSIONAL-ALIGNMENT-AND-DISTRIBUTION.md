# Phase 12.1 — Professional Alignment and Distribution

Phase 12.1 extends the real Yaposan Publisher editor with a professional multi-object alignment engine.

## Included
- Align to selection, page, page margins, or a key object
- Left, horizontal center, right, top, vertical middle, and bottom alignment
- Horizontal and vertical distribution
- Exact equal-gap spacing
- Match width, height, or both dimensions
- Match X, Y, or both positions
- Center selections on the page or inside margins
- Keep selected objects inside page bounds
- Locked-object protection
- Layout ribbon and Alignment Manager integration
- Undo/redo, save/load, and export-model compatibility
