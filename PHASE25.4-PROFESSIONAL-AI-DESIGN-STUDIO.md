# Phase 25.4 — Professional AI Design Studio

Phase 25.4 integrates a provider-ready, human-controlled AI design workspace into the real Yaposan editor.

## Included
- AI layout generation and layout variations
- AI brand direction generation with palettes, typography, tone, and keywords
- AI image-generation request adapter with provider separation and safe credential boundaries
- AI content suggestions for headlines, body copy, captions, and calls to action
- AI design assistant workflows
- Design quality audit for contrast, fonts, alignment, overflow, image resolution, and brand consistency
- Editable, non-destructive outputs with review-before-apply guardrails
- Brand profile integration and production-aware recommendations
- New AI Design ribbon and integrated modal workspace
- Tests and Phase 25.4 verification command

External generative providers require user-supplied configuration. API credentials are intentionally not embedded in project documents or source defaults.
