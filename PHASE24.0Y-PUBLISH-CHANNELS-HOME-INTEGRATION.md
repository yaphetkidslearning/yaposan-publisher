# Phase 24.0Y - Publish Channels Home Integration

Adds a new Publish Channels section under Recent Projects on the home page.

Channels included:
- eBay
- Etsy
- Shopify
- Amazon
- Facebook Marketplace
- Instagram Shop
- TikTok Shop
- Walmart Marketplace
- Pinterest Catalog

Each channel has a branded visual mark, responsive 3D card, connection status, and navigation into the Marketplace workspace. Existing home-page layout, dark/light theme, sidebar, routes, and Phase 24 functionality are preserved.
