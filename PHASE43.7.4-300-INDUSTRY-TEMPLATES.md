# Phase 43.7.4 — 300 Additional Phase 24.3D Industry Templates

Adds 30 unique, fully editable templates in each category: Business, Marketing, Social Media, Real Estate, Restaurant, Healthcare, Education, Technology, Fashion, and Sports.

All templates use PublisherPage and PublisherElement, curated typography and palettes, geometric layouts, no-people imagery, appropriate print/digital sizing, live page previews, searchable metadata, and unique IDs/names.
