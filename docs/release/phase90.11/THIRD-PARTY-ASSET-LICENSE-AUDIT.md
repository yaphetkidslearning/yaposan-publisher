# Third-Party Asset and License Audit

Open-source licensing of Yaposan source code does not automatically grant redistribution rights for bundled fonts, stock images, templates, icons, audio/video, datasets or third-party generated resources.

Before public release, inventory every distributable asset and dependency. Record its source, license, attribution requirements, modification/redistribution rights and whether commercial/public redistribution is permitted. Anything with unclear rights must be removed, replaced, or excluded from the public repository/package before certification.
