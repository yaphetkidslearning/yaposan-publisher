# Phase 43.7.5 — 320 Additional Publication Templates

Phase 43.7.5 extends the existing 1,685-template Phase 43.7.4 library with 320 additional Phase 24.3D-style publication templates.

## Added

- 20 Letterheads
- 20 Envelopes
- 20 Certificates
- 20 Invoices
- 20 Brochures
- 20 Newsletters
- 20 Flyers
- 20 Posters
- 20 Menus
- 20 Labels
- 20 Packaging templates
- 20 Calendars
- 20 Book Covers
- 20 Resumes
- 20 Presentation Covers
- 20 Social Media Kits

The 300 industry templates from Phase 43.7.4 remain preserved and integrated. The resulting library contains 2,005 templates.

All new templates use editable PublisherPage and PublisherElement objects, publication-ready dimensions, professional typography, curated palettes, no-people image placeholders, unique IDs and names, search metadata, and live browser previews.
