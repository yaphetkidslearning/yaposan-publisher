# Phase 54 — Enterprise Brand Governance & Content Operations

Phase 54 adds a governed brand and creative-operations foundation to Yaposan.

## Included

- Typed brand asset, policy, and content-request models
- Governed brand library for logos, colors, fonts, icons, images, and templates
- Approved, review, restricted, and retired lifecycle states
- Brand version and ownership metadata
- Brand rule engine with blocker, warning, and guidance severities
- Creative-request intake and production status tracking
- Brand health scoring and blocker reporting
- AsyncStorage persistence
- Home navigation integration
- Phase-specific tests

## Honest external requirements

Enterprise DAM synchronization, remote directory groups, SSO policy enforcement, cloud approvals, and organization-wide remote policy delivery require configured external services. Phase 54 provides the local application foundation and does not claim those providers are deployed.
