# Phase 43.4.9 — 225 Professional Templates

This update adds 100 new professional templates to the Phase 43 collection.

## Totals
- Previous templates: 125
- New templates: 100
- Total templates: 225

## Method
The new templates use the same direct Publisher object architecture established in Phase 24.3D:
- editable `PublisherPage` and `PublisherElement` objects
- professional photography
- strong editorial geometry
- controlled typography and palettes
- no flattened full-page artwork

The 100 templates are distributed across Business, Marketing, Social Media, Real Estate, Restaurant, Healthcare, Education, Events, Fashion, and Technology.
