# Phase 91.17 — SEO Cleanup, How Yaposan Works, FAQ & Contact

## Release goals
- Remove stale Phase 90.14 title text so no legacy postprocessor can restore the old title.
- Keep authentication pages out of the public sitemap and mark Sign In/Register as `noindex,nofollow`.
- Add public, indexable `/help` and `/contact` pages to the sitemap.
- Replace the old short Learning Center with a practical "How Yaposan Works" onboarding experience.
- Provide direct in-app links to Home, Settings, AI Provider Settings, Photo Studio, Publisher, Web Studio, Presentation Studio, Agent Studio, Projects, Diagnostics, and Contact.
- Add FAQ/Q&A for common first-use questions.
- Add a Contact & Support section without pretending a support backend exists before one is configured.

## Support delivery boundary
The Contact page intentionally does not claim that a support ticket is sent. A future release can connect email, CRM, service desk, or ticket APIs to the existing UI.
