## Construction method

Every new design follows the same method used in Phase 24.3D:

- Direct `PublisherPage` and `PublisherElement` objects
- Strong page-level composition before decoration
- One professional image used as a main visual anchor
- Oversized editable typography
- Limited, intentional color palettes
- Purposeful rectangles, circles, borders, and overlays
- Fully editable text, images, shapes, positions, colors, and sizing
- No flattened finished-template background

The additions are registered through `phase43ProfessionalTemplateEcosystem.ts` and are available in the normal template browser and search system.
