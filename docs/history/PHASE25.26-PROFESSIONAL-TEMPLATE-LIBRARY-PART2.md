# Phase 25.26 - Professional Template Library Part 2

Phase 25.26 adds 100 new editable templates to the active browser.

## New collections

- 10 Premium Brochures
- 10 Annual Reports
- 10 Magazines
- 10 Newsletters
- 10 Event Flyers
- 10 Posters
- 10 Resumes and CVs
- 10 Portfolios
- 10 Infographics
- 10 Catalogs

## Integration

The new `PHASE2526_TEMPLATES` collection is imported by `builtInTemplates.ts`, appended to `ALL_PROFESSIONAL_TEMPLATES`, and registered through the existing professional template registry. Each template contains editable Publisher elements and searchable metadata.
