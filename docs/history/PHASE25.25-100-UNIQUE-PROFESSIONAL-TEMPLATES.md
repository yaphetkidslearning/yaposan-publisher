# Phase 25.25 — 100 Unique Professional Templates

Phase 25.25 adds 100 new editable templates to the active template browser.

## Added
- 10 Business templates
- 10 Marketing templates
- 10 Social Media templates
- 10 Print templates
- 10 Education templates
- 10 Healthcare templates
- 10 Real Estate templates
- 10 Restaurant templates
- 10 Church templates
- 10 Technology templates

The collection uses ten substantially different composition systems: split panel, hero, sidebar, horizontal bands, modular cards, editorial, diagonal, grid, minimalist, and poster. Layout assignment rotates by category so each category contains a varied set rather than recolors of one design.

All templates are registered in `ALL_PROFESSIONAL_TEMPLATES`, searchable, editable, and visible in the existing template browser.
