# Phase 25.9 — Enterprise Security & Compliance

Phase 25.9 adds a production-oriented security, compliance, identity, and governance subsystem to the real Yaposan editor.

## Included

- Enterprise Security ribbon and management center
- Identity and role visibility
- MFA and trusted-device controls
- Member suspension and restoration
- Project and export encryption policies
- Restricted external sharing controls
- Publishing security gates
- Audit-event tracking and resolution
- Session and retention policies
- SOC 2, GDPR, and ISO 27001 readiness models
- Security scoring, remediation guidance, and governance reporting
- Adapter-ready architecture for enterprise identity providers and policy services

All earlier Phase 25.0–25.8 capabilities remain integrated.
