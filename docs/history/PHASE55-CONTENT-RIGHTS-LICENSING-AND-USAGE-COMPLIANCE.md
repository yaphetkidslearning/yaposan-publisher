# Phase 55 - Content Rights, Licensing & Usage Compliance

Phase 55 adds a local, evidence-oriented rights management foundation for Yaposan.

## Included

- Licensed asset registry
- Source, owner, license scope, channel, territory, and expiration tracking
- Cleared, review, restricted, and expired states
- Usage approval queue
- Attribution requirements
- High-risk rights rules
- Compliance scoring and blocker reporting
- AsyncStorage persistence
- Rights & Licensing dashboard
- Home navigation integration

## External requirements

Stock-provider APIs, contract repositories, automated legal review, remote takedown services, and authoritative license verification require configured external services. Phase 55 does not fabricate those connections.
