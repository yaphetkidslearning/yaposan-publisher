# Phase 24.2A.1 — Professional Editable Business Card Template Library

This release expands the Phase 24.2A.0 template engine with 120 real editable, two-sided business card templates.

## Included
- 120 templates generated from 12 industry collections and 10 distinct visual systems.
- Front and back pages for every template.
- Editable placeholders for person name, title, business name, phone, email, website, address, logo, and QR code.
- Correct 3.5 × 2 inch business-card canvas with bleed and margins.
- Free and premium metadata, featured and trending flags, search tags, and industry filters.
- Professional template browser with search, industry filters, favorites, details, and template counts.
- One-click `Use template` creates a normal Publisher project, saves it, and opens it in the editor.

## Industry collections
Corporate, Technology, Real Estate, Healthcare, Restaurant, Construction, Church, Fashion, Automotive, Beauty, Finance, and Creative.

## Design systems
Modern, Minimal, Luxury, Bold, Elegant, Geometric, Clean, Premium, Classic, and Creative.
