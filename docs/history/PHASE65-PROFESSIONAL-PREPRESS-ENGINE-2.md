# Phase 65 - Professional Prepress Engine 2

Phase 65 adds a unified press-readiness and production-certification layer on top of Yaposan's rendering, vector, raster, typography, layout, color, and export engines.

## Delivered
- Press profiles for offset, digital, flexographic, screen, and large-format workflows
- Bleed, trim, and safe-zone inspection
- Effective image resolution and image color-space checks
- Font embedding, outlining, and restricted-font validation
- Hairline and minimum printable line-width detection
- Total ink coverage validation
- Process and spot-color registration checks
- Overprint and knockout conflict analysis
- Transparency compatibility checks
- Automatic spread, choke, and no-trap planning
- Process and spot separation plate generation
- Soft-proof plans with paper, black-ink, overprint, and ink-limit simulation
- Deterministic production certificates and checksums
- Complete prepress workflow orchestration and Phase 65 regression tests
