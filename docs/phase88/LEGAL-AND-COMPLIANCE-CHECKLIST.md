# Legal and Compliance Checklist

Required before accepting public accounts or payments:

- Terms of Service reviewed by qualified counsel
- Privacy Policy and data-retention disclosures
- Cookie Policy and consent behavior where required
- Acceptable Use and AI-content policies
- Refund, cancellation, and subscription terms
- Copyright/DMCA process
- Enterprise Data Processing Agreement
- App-store privacy labels and declarations

Generated drafts are not a substitute for legal review.
