# Open-Source and Hosted-Service Boundary

## Open source
The source code covered by the repository's MIT License may be used, modified, and distributed under that license. Contributors can develop and test the public application without receiving Yaposan production credentials.

## Hosted services
A public source repository does not expose or transfer production credentials, customer data, payment-provider accounts, private deployment infrastructure, managed databases, private signing keys, or third-party service credentials. Some hosted capabilities can require accounts, provider keys, usage credits, or services operated separately from the open-source code.

## AI providers
Local AI and bring-your-own-provider paths should remain clearly distinguished from Yaposan-hosted AI or credit-backed services. Provider terms and usage charges remain subject to the relevant provider.

## Assets and third-party rights
The MIT License applies only where Yaposan has the right to license the material. Third-party fonts, icons, images, models, templates, datasets, and other assets retain their own licenses and terms. Contributors must document compatible rights for newly added third-party assets.
