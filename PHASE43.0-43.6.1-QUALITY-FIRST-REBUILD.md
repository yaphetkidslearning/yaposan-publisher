# Phase 43.0–43.6.1 Quality-First Rebuild

This rebuild raises the first template collection to a professional art-direction standard and consolidates the home sidebar into ten expandable sections.

## Template quality
- 32 categories
- 5 intentionally different templates per category
- 160 original editable templates
- Five composition systems: editorial, split, framed, geometric, and photo-led
- Evidence-based quality audit covering typography, palette, composition, metadata, editability, and export readiness
- Design-review badge in the Template Center

## Sidebar
The sidebar now exposes only ten primary sections. Each section opens its related tools, reducing visual clutter while preserving access to the full platform.

## Important
The templates are original Yaposan designs. Canva was used only as a reference for marketplace organization and quality expectations.
