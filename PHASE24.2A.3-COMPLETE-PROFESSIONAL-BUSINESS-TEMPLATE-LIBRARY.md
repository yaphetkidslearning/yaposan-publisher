# Phase 24.2A.3 — Complete Professional Business Template Library

Phase 24.2A.3 completes the original Phase 24.2A business-library target and is cumulative with 24.2A.0 and 24.2A.1.

## Included editable templates

- 120 front-and-back business cards
- 100 letterheads
- 80 invoices
- 40 quotes and estimates
- 40 receipts
- 40 certificates
- 80 company profiles
- 70 brochures
- 70 business flyers

**Total: 640 editable templates.**

All templates are generated as real Publisher pages and elements. They include smart placeholders, editable text, shapes, logo regions, QR regions, theme-ready colors, and print dimensions. Invoice, quote, and receipt layouts also include editable table objects.

## Integration

The built-in template registry loads both the existing business-card library and the new document library. The professional template browser can search, filter, favorite, preview, and create Publisher projects from these templates.
