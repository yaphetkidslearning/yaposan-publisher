# Yaposan Governance

Yaposan uses a maintainer-led, community-informed governance model.

## Roles
- **Contributors** submit issues, documentation, designs, tests, translations, and pull requests.
- **Maintainers** review changes, protect release quality, and merge approved work.
- **Security maintainers** coordinate private vulnerability reports and security releases.

## Decisions
Routine changes are decided through pull-request review. Significant architecture, compatibility, licensing, security, or community-policy changes should begin as a GitHub Discussion or tracked proposal. Maintainers seek consensus; when consensus is not practical, maintainers make the final decision and document the rationale.

## Becoming a maintainer
Sustained constructive contributions, sound review judgment, respectful community participation, and familiarity with the project may lead to maintainer nomination. Existing maintainers approve new maintainers.

## Inactive maintainers
Access may be reduced after extended inactivity for security and operational safety. Returning maintainers may request restoration.

## Commercial independence
Open-source contribution does not grant access to Yaposan production credentials, customer data, billing systems, private infrastructure, or hosted-service secrets.
