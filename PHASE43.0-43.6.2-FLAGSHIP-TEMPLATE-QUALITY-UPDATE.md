# Phase 43.0-43.6.2 - Flagship Template Quality Update

This update prioritizes demonstrable design quality over template count.

## Added

- 20 original commercial-quality flagship templates.
- Five Business Flyers.
- Five Business Cards.
- Five tri-fold Brochures.
- Five two-page Presentation decks.
- Fully editable Publisher elements rather than raster screenshots.
- Distinct art direction, typography, spacing, palettes and content hierarchy for every flagship design.
- Quality score 100 metadata for the reviewed flagship collection.
- Rich flagship preview treatment in the Template Center.

## Quality requirements

Each flagship template includes structured typography, a curated five-color palette, at least one strong composition system, editable shapes and text, clear calls to action, and production-sized pages. Presentation templates contain a cover and agenda page. Brochures use a real three-panel layout.

The remaining category library is preserved, but the four flagship categories now use the new Phase 43.6.2 designs.
