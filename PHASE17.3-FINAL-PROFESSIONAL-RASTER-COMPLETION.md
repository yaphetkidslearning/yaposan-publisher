# Phase 17.3 — Final Professional Raster Completion

Phase 17.3 completes the Phase 17 raster/photo architecture with RAW development, HDR merge settings, frequency separation, panorama/focus/exposure stacking, LUT grading, metadata, multi-recipe delivery, render manifests, audit integration, save/load fields, Photo Studio controls, and automated tests.
