# Phase 43.0–43.6.1 — Quality-First Professional Template Ecosystem

This update replaces the quantity-first Phase 43 generator with a smaller, art-directed premium collection.

## Included

- 32 professional categories
- 5 original templates per category
- 160 editable templates total
- Five genuinely different composition systems per category: editorial, split, framed, geometric and photo-led
- Category-specific headlines and supporting copy
- Professional font pairings and curated palettes
- Improved marketplace preview cards that reflect each composition
- Quality score floor of 98
- Existing editor creation workflow preserved

Templates are original Yaposan designs. The reference image was used only for information architecture and expected presentation quality.
