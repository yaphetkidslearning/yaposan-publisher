# Yaposan Community Code of Conduct

Yaposan is intended to be a welcoming, constructive, and technically serious open-source community.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully
- critique ideas and code rather than people
- avoid harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, and personal attacks
- respect privacy and confidential information
- give credit for others' work
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest
- avoid intentionally disruptive behavior, spam, and malicious contributions
- follow project security and contribution rules

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions and discussions that violate these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent removal from project spaces.

For sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's available private contact or security channels rather than posting personal information publicly.
