- normal `PublisherPage` objects
- normal editable `PublisherElement` objects
- direct text, rectangle, and image helpers
- professional photography
- controlled palettes
- deliberate typography and page composition
- existing template loader and editor compatibility

No flattened image or duplicate replacement format was introduced.
