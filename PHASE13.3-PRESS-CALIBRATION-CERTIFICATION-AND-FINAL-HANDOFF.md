# Phase 13.3 — Press Calibration, Certification & Final Handoff

Completes the Phase 13 production workflow with press/substrate targeting, screening and dot-gain controls, small/reversed text validation, hairline detection, deterministic production fingerprints, approval states, and final press certification.

## Included
- Digital, sheetfed offset, web offset, and wide-format press targets
- Coated, uncoated, newsprint, synthetic, and board substrates
- Screen frequency and dot-gain controls
- Minimum text and reverse-text validation
- Minimum hairline enforcement
- Black-text overprint policy
- Draft, internal, client, and press approval states
- Stable production fingerprint for handoff verification
- Final certification status in the Prepress Manager and report
